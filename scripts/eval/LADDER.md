# THE POST-CONVERGENCE SMARTNESS LADDER

His order, 30 Aug. **Strictly after the bar is met** — none of this begins
before two straight runs at 48/50, zero guard breaches, latency inside budget,
and hold-outs trending with the visible bank.

1. **FILES FEED EVERY ANSWER — knowing.** Once Client Files exist, every brain
   answer about a person draws on their file, not just their rows.
2. **THE MORNING BRIEF NOTICES.** Ranks the roster unprompted, surface-only: it
   points, it never acts. Who is slipping, who is surging, who is owed a touch.
3. **HIS THUMB IS THE TEACHER.** Log every edit he makes to a draft before
   sending. A weekly edit-pattern digest feeds the drafting voice so drafts
   converge on how he actually writes. Edits are data; nothing sends itself.
4. **HIS CONTENT CORPUS BECOMES DOCTRINE.** Ingested and retrievable, so Jim
   answers clients in his philosophy, citing what he has actually taught and
   never inventing coaching he never gave.

**Cross-cutting: two-tier models.** The small fast one by default, escalation to
the big one by WRITTEN rules — long reasoning, drafting, ambiguity — with spend
reported per run as always.

**Laws unchanged throughout.** Only tools state facts. Nothing sends unattended.
Guard cases are never traded for fluency. The bar comes before any of this.
