Privacy Policy

Updated 16 September 2026

Who this is

YOURJIMBFF is a coaching app run by Yusuf Richardson. If you want to reach a human about anything on this page, email yusuf@yourjimbff.com, or text Yusuf on the number he coaches you from.

What the app holds

What you tell us when you set up: your name, your phone number, your date of birth, your height, your weight, your goal weight, how and how often you train, and anything you write in your own words.

What you log day to day: food — typed, photographed, or read off a label — workouts and sets, weigh-ins, steps, sleep, energy check-ins, journal entries and progress photos.

What you say: your messages to Jim, the assistant inside the app, and anything you send Yusuf.

How you get in: your access code, and a sign-in token your own phone keeps so you are not asked for the code every time.

What it does not hold: your card number. Payments run through Stripe and card details never touch this app.

This is health information, and it is treated that way

Your weight, your body, your food, your sleep and your progress photos are health information about you. It is the most sensitive thing in here and it is the reason the rest of this page exists.

It is never sold. It is never used for advertising. It is never handed to an employer, an insurer or a data broker. There are no advertising trackers and no analytics SDKs anywhere in this app.

What it is used for

One thing: coaching you.

That means showing you your own numbers, working out your targets, writing and adjusting your programme, letting Yusuf read your logs so that he is coaching off something real, and sending you the occasional text about your own account.

Who else touches it

The app is built on other people’s infrastructure, and being honest about your data means naming them.

Supabase — the database, and the private storage your photos live in.

Netlify — hosting, and the small server functions that sign you in and delete your account.

Anthropic — the AI. When you describe or photograph a meal or a workout, or send a message to Jim, that text or image goes to Anthropic’s API so it can come back as numbers and answers. It is sent through the API, which is not used to train their models.

Stripe — payments, if you are on a paid plan. They hold the card details; this app never sees them.

Resend — the occasional email the app sends.

USDA FoodData Central — public nutrition lookups. It is given the name of a food and nothing at all about you.

Apple Health — your step count, and only if you switch that on yourself.

Text messages come from Yusuf’s own phone. There is no SMS company sitting in the middle of them.

Who can see you inside the app

You, and Yusuf, who is your coach.

Other clients cannot see your logs, your photos, your messages or your name. That is enforced by the database itself — every table checks who is asking before it answers — and not by what a screen happens to show.

How long it is kept

For as long as your account is open. When you delete your account it goes, and what that covers is listed below.

Backups held by our database provider may keep a copy for a short window before they roll over.

What you can do about it

See it. Most of it is already on your own screens; ask for the rest.

Correct it. In Settings, or tell Yusuf.

Take it with you. Ask and you will be sent a copy.

Delete it. Settings, at the bottom: "Delete my account and all my data". It removes your logs, your photos, your messages and your account. It cannot be undone, and it only ever touches your own data.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights — GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA and CPRA and the like. Email yusuf@yourjimbff.com and they will be honoured.

Security, said plainly

Everything travels encrypted. Every table is locked so that a request can only return your own rows, and your photos sit in a private bucket that needs your own signed session to open.

The honest part: your access code is what gets you in, so treat it like a password. Do not share it, and tell Yusuf if you think somebody else has it.

Children

This app is not for anyone under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian has to agree to this on your behalf.

If this changes

If anything here changes in a way that matters, this screen comes back and you will be asked to read and agree to it again, exactly as you were the first time.
